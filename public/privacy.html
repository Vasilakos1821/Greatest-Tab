Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how "Greatest Tab" (the "Application") handles your data. The Application is built as a static, client-side browser extension and web page.

1. Data Collection and Local Storage

The Application does not have a backend server or database. All user preferences, sticky notes, custom shortcuts, active clock settings, and custom wallpaper images are stored strictly on your local device using your browser's localStorage mechanism. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any external servers.

2. Google Calendar Data and Limited Use Policy

To provide the calendar synchronization feature, the Application requests read-only access to your Google Calendar.

  • How we use it: The Application fetches your calendar events strictly to display them on your local dashboard interface.
  • How we store it: Your calendar events are temporarily cached in your browser's local storage to prevent unnecessary API calls and are wiped if you clear your browser data.
  • Limited Use Disclosure: New Tab Dashboard's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not share, sell, or transfer your Google Calendar data to any third-party services, nor do humans read this data.

3. Third-Party APIs

The Application makes direct client-side requests to public third-party APIs to fetch specific widgets:

  • Open-Meteo: Receives your geographic coordinates (if you grant browser location permissions) solely to return local weather data.
  • NASA APOD API: Accessed via public endpoints to display the space article of the day.
  • Saurav.tech News API: Accessed to pull current news headlines.
  • Spotify Embed: Uses standard iframe embeds governed by Spotify's privacy policy.

4. Changes to this Policy

If the Application integrates new features that change how local data or third-party APIs are handled, this document will be updated. Because the Application is entirely client-side, we cannot notify you directly; please review this page periodically.

5. Contact

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us via our GitHub Repository.
